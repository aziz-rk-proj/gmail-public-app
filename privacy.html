Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Gmail Reader App ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use your data when you connect your Google Account.

2. Data We Collect

We request access to your Gmail data via Google OAuth APIs, specifically the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope. This allows us to read your email profile and messages.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your Gmail data is accessed solely to display your inbox messages directly within the application interface. We do NOT store, sell, or share your personal emails or Google user data on any external servers.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Deletion

You can revoke access to our application at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.
